The cloud is indicative: its shape is a Gaussian plume from a virtual ground-level source at the station (wind, Pasquill stability class, dry deposition), and its intensity is the measured concentration relative to the 95th percentile of the test period. It is not an emission-based model or a concentration field. Forecasts are validated retrospectively for CO and SO₂ only; no threshold warnings or health advice are issued.
